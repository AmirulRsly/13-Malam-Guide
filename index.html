00The basics

You came out to a lake house for a fishing holiday, from the 19th to the 31st of October. That's 13 days. The first three nights are quiet. From night 4 one of three ghosts comes each night, never the same one two nights running. It arrives between 20:30 and 22:00 and leaves at 03:00, unless you put it to rest. On the last night all three come at once. There are three endings:

Good endingFix the car and drive out through the gate.
True endingSecret. Hidden at the bottom of this page.
Bad endingsDie. Each one is named after whatever killed you.

Staying alive

  • Fish by day. Fish are your food and your money: Hamid the merchant trades only for fish. Cook them on the stove in the house.
  • Listen to the radio. The weather channel tells you about rain and storms. The midnight ghost-story show (Cerita Seram Tengah Malam) tells you what each ghost is and how the old folk kept it away.
  • Prepare before dusk. Each ghost has two tasks. Do both and your weapons hurt it properly. Do one and it takes twice as many hits. Do none and weapons don't touch it, so hide (wardrobe, outhouse, bushes, tall weeds).
  • Scavenge. The forest behind the house is laid out new every morning: materials, mushrooms, beetles for bait, and sometimes a small chest. The empty neighbour houses have storage boxes worth opening.

PART ONE: THE GOOD ENDING

01The car won't start

For the first couple of days it's just a holiday, and the car is fine. On day 3 Hamid comes by to warn you. The morning after that you decide to leave, and the car won't start. Try it twice and the journal lists what it needs.

Car parts you find before then are no use. Once the car has broken down, you're collecting five parts, plus the toolbox to fit them and the car jack for the tyre.

Getting into the houses

  • Keys are named after what they open: Lake house key, East house key, Back room key, Balcony room key, Forest house key, Boatman's house key.
  • Big house: read the owner's notes. The Balcony room key is in the mailbox by the road gate. The Back room key is lying somewhere in the house's open rooms.
  • Look under stones, up on roofs and in what you fish up. Treasure from the lake can include a house key.
  • A locked door can also be smashed with a sledgehammer, if you have one.

Crossing the lake

  1. Take the fuel can out of the boat first.
  2. Make oars at the workbench: 2 wood planks + 2 rope.
  3. E on the boat fits the oars, and E again gets in. W/S row, A/D turn, and E gets you out next to a bank or the jetty.
  4. Row over to the far woods and take the tyre down from the swing.

03The battery: Hamid's trade

When the car breaks down with Hamid there, he offers to bring a battery from his house. From then on it's at the top of his trade list. It costs two particular fish:

A Baung over 18 inBaung bite in the early evening, roughly 17:00 to 20:00.
A Haruan under 16 inHaruan only bite in a storm. Check the radio's forecast.
Fish you catch keep their length. Check it in your fish book (K) before you trade. Hamid doesn't come on rainy or stormy days, so hold on to that storm Haruan until a dry day.

04Fixing the car and escaping

  1. With the toolbox and a part in your bag, go to the car and fit the part. It's a skill-check minigame. Missed checks cost time and make a loud bang that ghosts can hear.
  2. The spare tyre also needs the car jack.
  3. Work in daylight and on a full stomach, because hunger slows the work. In the haze, car work goes slower too.
  4. Fit the fifth part and the car starts. You crash through the gate and drive away: GOOD ENDING, ESCAPE.
Do it before night 13. On the last night all three ghosts come at once, nothing kills them, nowhere hides you, and one blow is the end.
PART TWO: THE TRUE ENDING

Spoilers ahead

The true ending is the game's secret. This part gives away the hidden boss, the twist about a character, and how every ghost is put to rest for good. Try to find it yourself first.

05The goal

The thing in the lake is the Mambang Air, the lake spirit. Someone has been feeding it fish, and people, for years. While it lives the ghosts keep coming back. Kill it.

To call it up, lay out four things on the jetty and be there between 00:00 and 01:00:

A bodyHamid's, dragged out onto the jetty
Pontianak hairleft when she's put to rest
Toyol earleft when it's put to rest
Pocong shroudleft when it's put to rest
All of this has to be done before night 13. On the last night the ghosts can't be put to rest.

06The boatman's book (the lore)

The ritual is written in the Mambang Air book in the boatman's house on the far shore. You don't need to read it, but it's how the game tells you all this.

  1. Row across (see crossing the lake).
  2. The house is locked, because a toyol stole the key. Hide in the outhouse behind it and read the note pinned on the wall to your right.
  3. Once you've read the note, the next haunted night is always a toyol. Put it to rest and it drops the Boatman's house key. On the last day the door is found broken in.
  4. The book is on his shelf. Hold it and press LMB to read it.

07Putting the ghosts to rest

Beating a ghost down with weapons only drives it off, and it leaves nothing. To get its remains you have to finish it the old way, on a night you prepared for. When it's put to rest it comes apart in smoke and its remains fall to the ground. Pick them up.

Pontianak → hair

Signs: frangipani smell, a baby crying (faint means close)
  • Do both tasks: chop the banana plants, and burn the white blossoms.
  • Get the Pontianak spike. It's in the forest from day 1 (in a new spot each day until you take it), and there's one in the east house.
  • Hold the spike and press LMB to stab her in the nape of the neck from behind. While she's stunned after a charge, it works from any side.

Toyol → ear

Signs: giggling, little footsteps, food going missing
  • Salt the doorways, put all food away, and find the child's toy.
  • Leave the toy out by the door. When the toyol sits down to play with it, hit it. Off the toy it's too quick to hit.

Pocong → shroud

Signs: heavy knocking, a hopping shape in white
  • Untie the knot on the fresh grave (E), and drop every shroud strip back onto the grave (G).
  • The killing blow has to land on its head, where the knot is.

08Hamid

The friendly merchant is the lake spirit's keeper. He broke your car, and you were going to be victim number six. His body is the one the ritual needs.

  • He can't be attacked on days 3 and 4. On other days, use the shotgun, the machete or the sledgehammer.
  • Killing him curses the lake: its fish are gone for good. Only the forest pond is left to fish. Do it last.
  • His body holds his confession note, a sledgehammer and his goods, including a car battery if he was carrying one.
  • Hold E on the body to drag it, and haul it out onto the jetty boards. A body left there stays put.

09The summoning

  1. Get Hamid's body onto the jetty and let go of it.
  2. Drop the hair, the ear and the shroud on the jetty boards (G).
  3. Stand on the jetty, or on the shore right by it, between 00:00 and 01:00.

The water goes still and the offering slides into the lake. Bloody weeds rise in a ring around you, so bring everything you need before midnight.

10The fight

Bring: the shotgun and as many shells as you can carry, the machete, your fishing rod, and medicine or food.

Phase 1: on land

It wades ashore, claws up close and spits blood from range, and there's no hiding. Keep moving and shoot. About 15 shotgun hits drive it back into the lake. Machete cuts count for less.

Phase 2: in the water

It swims under the surface and leaps across in front of you like a dolphin, spitting from the top of the arc. Only hits while it's in the air count. It takes about 10. It gets faster and spits more as it's hurt.

If you're down to fewer than 10 shells, boxes of them sometimes float up. Cast your rod at one to pull it in.

Phase 3: the chant

It goes out to the middle of the lake and chants under the water. Cast your rod at it, hook it and reel it in onto the jetty, then finish it.

The red lifts, the sun comes up, and you get the TRUE ENDING: THE EXORCIST.

11Checklist

  • Pontianak spike found
  • Pontianak put to rest with the spike → hair
  • Toyol put to rest on its toy → ear
  • Pocong put to rest with the last blow to its head → shroud
  • Shotgun, plenty of shells, rod, healing
  • Hamid killed (not on days 3–4) and dragged onto the jetty
  • Remains on the jetty, and you there between 00:00 and 01:00
  • All before night 13
